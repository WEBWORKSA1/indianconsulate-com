What changed in 2026

The OCI went digital. Here’s what that means for you.

  • e-OCI for new applicants. Missions announced in July 2026 that new OCI applicants receive a digital e-OCI; existing physical cards stay valid.
  • Online filing. The Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2026 moved applications, renewals and renunciations online at ociservices.gov.in.
  • Three-month update window. Update your OCI record free within three months of a new passport; later updates attract a fee.
  • Minors and two passports. The rules now state plainly that a minor cannot hold an Indian and a foreign passport at the same time.

Stay safe

Spot a fake “visa” or “consulate” site

Official Indian government portals use the .gov.in domain. Official sites warn that agents and intermediaries are not authorised to charge fees for visas or the e-Arrival card, and missions have reported spoofed phone numbers being used to demand money.
  • Never pay a caller claiming to be from an embassy.
  • Check the URL before you type card details.
  • Government fees are paid on the official portal or at the mission’s named application centre.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is IndianConsulate.com an official government website?

No. We are an independent, privately run information service. We are not affiliated with the Government of India or any embassy or consulate, and we never collect government fees. We link you to the official .gov.in portals to apply.

Can I renew my Indian passport from abroad?

Yes. Indian citizens abroad apply through the Passport Seva portal for Indian missions and then submit to their mission or its application centre. See our passport renewal guide.

Do I need a surrender certificate for an OCI?

If you held an Indian passport and later acquired foreign citizenship, a surrender (or renunciation) certificate is normally required. Our surrender certificate guide explains when and how.

Is my existing physical OCI card still valid after the e-OCI launch?

Mission notices state that existing physical OCI cards remain valid; the digital e-OCI is issued to new applicants, and existing cardholders can also keep a digital copy.

All guides and tools are free. The first checklist review is free and without obligation. If you choose hands-on help from an independent specialist, they quote you before any work begins.
