The rule in one sentence

Under the Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2026, a minor cannot hold an Indian passport and a foreign passport at the same time. Parents used to sign a declaration to that effect; now it is a stated rule, and airlines and immigration check it.

How most families decide

  • Born in a citizenship-by-birth country (US, Canada) and settling there? Most families choose Route B — the child already has a strong passport, and the OCI covers India.
  • Planning to return to India within a few years? Route A can be simpler — but if the child already has a foreign passport, the 2026 rule means that passport must be dealt with first.
  • Born in a country that doesn’t grant citizenship by birth? The child may only be eligible for Indian nationality, making Route A the default.

Documents for a minor’s OCI

  • Child’s foreign passport and birth certificate naming both parents
  • Parents’ passports and proof of Indian origin (or parents’ OCIs)
  • Consent/authorisation signed by both parents; custody orders where relevant
  • Parents’ marriage certificate
  • A square photo of the child meeting the upload spec — babies are hard; our photo checker helps

Remember the update rule

Children’s passports expire quickly. Each new foreign passport means an OCI update — free within three months. Put a reminder in your calendar the day the new passport arrives, or use our trip planner to generate one.
