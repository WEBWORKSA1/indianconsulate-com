Every Indian passport issued abroad goes through the same national system: an online application on the Passport Seva portal for Indian missions, verification, and printing. What changes from country to country is the front door — who takes your documents, whether you can post them, and how long the queue is. Missions outsource that front door to private application centres in many countries.

United States

  • Who: the Embassy in Washington and the consulates in New York, San Francisco, Chicago, Houston, Atlanta and Seattle, each covering a set of states.
  • Front door: VFS Global.
  • Typical flow: online Passport Seva form → VFS registration and fee → submit the packet as VFS instructs → track on VFS.
  • Watch out for: applying to the wrong consulate for your state of residence, and US immigration-status documents that are about to expire.

United Kingdom

  • Who: the High Commission in London and consulates in Birmingham and Edinburgh.
  • Front door: VFS Global.
  • Watch out for: proof of UK immigration status — with the UK’s move to digital eVisas, be ready to show your digital status evidence rather than an old physical card.

Canada

  • Who: the High Commission in Ottawa and consulates in Toronto and Vancouver, assigned by province or postal code.
  • Front door: BLS International. Services are submitted in person with biometrics, and BLS states that postal applications for visas and OCI are not accepted.
  • Extras: BLS offers an optional paid “premium lounge” that helps with form filling, uploads and photos; it says this does not speed up processing.
  • Watch out for: portal downtime notices — BLS Canada has asked applicants to reschedule appointments on dates when the passport portal is down.

United Arab Emirates

  • Who: the Embassy in Abu Dhabi and the Consulate General in Dubai (which covers Dubai and the Northern Emirates).
  • Front door: BLS International centres across the emirates.
  • Typical flow: online form → visit a BLS centre in person with original passport, Emirates ID / residence proof and photos.
  • Watch out for: heavy seasonal demand before school holidays, and announcements about changes of service provider or temporary closures — check the mission’s notices first.

Australia

  • Who: the High Commission in Canberra and consulates in Sydney, Melbourne and Perth.
  • Services: missions list passport, OCI, e-Visa, Emergency Certificates, life certificates, PCC and driving-licence verification among their consular services.
  • Watch out for: jurisdiction — confirm which post covers your state before applying.

Everywhere: the five delay-makers

  1. Name mismatches between your passport and supporting documents.
  2. Photo rejections — wrong size, shadows, off-white backgrounds. Use our photo checker.
  3. Police verification back in India for some cases.
  4. Expired local status documents.
  5. Holiday closures — missions close for Indian and local holidays.
