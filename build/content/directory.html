Embassy, High Commission or Consulate — what’s the difference?

Embassy

India’s main diplomatic mission in a non-Commonwealth country, usually in the capital. Often also handles consular work for its local region.

High Commission

The equivalent of an embassy in a Commonwealth country — e.g. London, Ottawa, Canberra, Singapore.

Consulate General

A regional office focused on consular services for a defined set of states or provinces — e.g. New York, Toronto, Dubai.
