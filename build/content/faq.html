FAQ

Frequently asked questions

About this website

Is this the official Indian consulate website?

No. IndianConsulate.com is an independent information service. Official applications are made on government portals ending in .gov.in and at your mission’s designated centre.

Do you charge for information?

No. Every guide and tool is free. We’re funded by labelled ads, sponsorships, donations and an optional help desk.

Can you speed up my application?

No one outside the government can legitimately speed up processing. Be wary of anyone who promises to.

OCI

What is an e-OCI?

A digital OCI issued to new applicants since July 2026, shown on your phone with your passport. Existing physical cards remain valid.

When must I update my OCI after a new passport?

Within three months to avoid the late fee. See the OCI guide.

Can I have an OCI and an Indian passport?

No. OCI is only for foreign citizens; India does not allow dual citizenship.

Passports

How early can I renew my Indian passport abroad?

Usually up to a year before expiry, or earlier if your pages run out or your details change.

I lost my passport abroad. What first?

File a police report, then contact the nearest mission. See emergencies.

Visas

Where do I apply for an Indian e-Visa?

Only on indianvisaonline.gov.in. Beware of look-alike sites.

Do OCI holders need an e-Arrival card?

Check the official portal for the current requirement; the form is free.

Citizenship & documents

I became a US/UK/Canadian citizen. Am I still Indian?

No — Indian citizenship ends when you voluntarily acquire another. Get a surrender certificate, then consider an OCI.

Do I need an apostille or embassy attestation?

It depends on where the document is from and where it will be used. See attestation & apostille.
