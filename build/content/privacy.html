Who we are

IndianConsulate.com (“we”, “us”) is an independent information website. You can reach us through our contact page.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through forms — for example name, email, country, phone (optional) and your message — when you request help, pledge support, enter a contest, apply for a role, subscribe or contact us. Please do not include passport, visa, bank or card numbers in forms.
  • Technical data — device, browser, pages visited and approximate location — collected through cookies and similar technologies by us and our advertising partners.
  • Tool inputs stay on your device. The photo checker, planner and checklist builder run in your browser. Uploaded photos are not sent to us; checklist progress is stored in your browser’s local storage.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and, where you ask, connect you with an independent documentation specialist who will contact you about that request.
  • To send alerts you opted into (unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run contests, process pledges and review job applications.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site and to show advertising.

Form processing

Form submissions are relayed by email through a third-party form service (FormSubmit) to our team. We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose and for our legitimate records, and we do not sell your personal information.
