Apostille or attestation?

India is a member of the Hague Apostille Convention (since 2005). That gives two main routes:

Your situationWhat you usually need
Indian document, to be used in another Apostille Convention countryAn apostille from India’s Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), after any required state or departmental verification
Indian document, to be used in a non-Apostille countryMEA attestation, then legalisation by that country’s embassy
Foreign document, to be used in India, issued in an Apostille countryAn apostille from the issuing country’s competent authority is generally sufficient
Foreign document from a non-Apostille country, or a document signed before an Indian consulAttestation by the Indian mission in that country

Common documents

  • Educational certificates — degrees and transcripts for jobs, licensing or study abroad. Usually need university or state verification before the MEA step.
  • Personal documents — birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates for visas, residence permits and name changes.
  • Powers of attorney — common for NRIs selling or managing property in India. Signed abroad, then attested by the mission or apostilled locally, then adjudicated (stamped) in India within the time allowed.
  • Affidavits and declarations — for passport, OCI and bank formalities.
  • Commercial documents — invoices, certificates of origin and company documents, typically via chambers of commerce.

Attestation at an Indian mission — how it works

  1. Check your mission’s list of documents it attests and whether it uses an outsourced centre.
  2. Prepare originals and copies, plus your passport and residence permit copies.
  3. Get translations into English where documents are in another language.
  4. Sign in front of the consular officer if the document is a declaration or power of attorney — don’t sign it beforehand.
  5. Pay the fee and collect, or arrange return courier.

Mistakes that cost weeks

  • Signing a power of attorney before the appointment
  • Skipping the state or university verification step before the MEA apostille
  • Assuming a notary’s stamp is enough for use abroad
  • Laminated documents — many authorities won’t stamp them
  • Name variations between the certificate and your passport without a linking affidavit

Frequently asked questions

Does an apostilled document need embassy attestation too?

Between Apostille Convention members, no — the apostille replaces legalisation. Some receiving authorities still have their own requirements, so confirm with whoever will receive the document.

Can someone in India get my documents apostilled for me?

Usually yes, with an authorisation letter and copies of your ID. Personal-document rules differ by state.

How long does it take?

From a few days for simple mission attestations to several weeks when state verification in India is involved. Build in buffer time before visa or job deadlines.
