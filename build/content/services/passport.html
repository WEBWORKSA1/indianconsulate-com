Where to applyThe Passport Seva portal for Indian missions (embassy.passportindia.gov.in), then your mission or its application centre
WhenYou can usually apply up to a year before expiry — earlier if pages run out or your details change
WhoIndian citizens only. If you have acquired another citizenship, you need a surrender certificate instead
Urgent?Many missions offer Tatkal (expedited) processing for an extra fee

Renewal, re-issue or replacement?

Indian missions treat almost every new passport for an existing holder as a re-issue. The reason you choose on the form matters, because it changes the documents required:

  • Validity expired or expiring — the standard case.
  • Pages exhausted — frequent travellers can apply before expiry.
  • Change in particulars — name after marriage or divorce, address, spouse’s name, appearance.
  • Lost, stolen or damaged — needs a police report and often extra verification. If you must travel immediately, see Emergency Certificates.

Step-by-step process

  1. Register on the Passport Seva portal for Indian missions and select the country where you live. Complete the online form carefully — your name must match your current passport exactly.
  2. Print and sign the completed form if your mission requires a printout.
  3. Follow your mission’s submission route. In the US and UK this runs through VFS Global; in Canada and the UAE through BLS International; elsewhere you may deal with the mission directly. Use the consulate finder to confirm yours.
  4. Book an appointment or prepare a postal packet as instructed, with original passport, documents and fees.
  5. Track your application on the centre’s tracker and the Passport Seva status page.
  6. Collect or receive the new passport. Your old passport is usually cancelled and returned.

Documents you will typically need

  • Completed online application
  • Current passport (original) plus copies of the first, last and any observation pages
  • Proof of legal status in your country — visa, residence permit or PR card
  • Proof of current local address
  • Recent photographs to the mission’s specification (usually 2×2 inches, white background)
  • Supporting documents for any change of particulars — marriage certificate, divorce decree, deed poll
  • For minors: birth certificate, both parents’ passports and a parental consent declaration

Why renewals get delayed

  • Police verification. Some applications need verification with police in India before or after issue, which can add weeks.
  • Name or date-of-birth mismatches between your old passport and supporting documents.
  • Photo rejections — shadows, a coloured background or the wrong size.
  • Holiday closures — missions close for both Indian and local holidays.
  • Incomplete status documents — an expired visa copy is a common one.

Passports for children born abroad

A child born abroad to Indian parents can be registered and issued an Indian passport through the mission. Under the Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2026, a minor cannot hold an Indian passport and a foreign passport at the same time. If your child already holds another country’s passport — for example through birthright citizenship — talk to your mission before applying, because the foreign passport may need to be surrendered or the OCI route may be the right one.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extend the validity of my current Indian passport?

No. Validity can’t be extended — you apply for a new passport (re-issue).

Can I stay abroad on an expired Indian passport?

Your residence status depends on the host country’s rules, but most require a valid passport. Renew well before expiry, especially if your visa or residence permit is tied to it.

Can I renew in a country where I’m only visiting?

Missions generally serve residents of their jurisdiction. Short-term visitors may face extra verification or be told to apply where they live. Ask the mission first.

How long does it take?

It varies by mission and case — from about a week for straightforward Tatkal cases to several weeks when police verification is needed. Use the trip planner to work out a start-by date.
