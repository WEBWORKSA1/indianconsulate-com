Apply only on the official portal: indianvisaonline.gov.in. The official portal itself warns that agents and intermediaries are not authorised to charge fees for visas or the e-Arrival card. Look-alike sites often add large mark-ups.

e-Visa categories

  • e-Tourist — sightseeing, visiting friends and family, short yoga or wellness programmes.
  • e-Business — meetings, sales, recruiting, trade fairs and technical visits (not employment).
  • e-Conference — attending conferences organised by government bodies or with required clearances.
  • e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant — treatment at a recognised hospital, and family members accompanying the patient.
  • Other e-categories listed on the portal (such as AYUSH treatment) for specific purposes.

How to apply for an e-Visa

  1. Check eligibility for your nationality and purpose on the official portal.
  2. Prepare your files: a square digital photo with a plain light background (test it with our photo checker), a scan of your passport bio page and any category documents.
  3. Complete the online form. You can save a partial application and come back with your temporary ID.
  4. Pay the government fee on the portal. Fees are non-refundable, even if the visa is refused.
  5. Receive your ETA by email, check every detail, and carry a printout.

Timing: apply at least four days before arrival — two to three weeks is safer. Your passport should be valid for at least six months from arrival with two blank pages.

The e-Arrival card

India’s e-Arrival card is a free online arrival form, submitted within 72 hours before you land. It is not a visa, and you should never pay a third party to file it. The government’s Indian Visa Su-Swagatam mobile app also supports visa services.

Regular visas

Employment, student, research, journalist, entry (X) and long-term visas are applied for through the Indian mission responsible for where you live — usually via its outsourced visa centre. Expect an in-person appointment, biometrics and longer processing than an e-Visa. Use the consulate finder to find your mission.

Common reasons for refusal or delay

  • A photo that isn’t square or has a shadowed, coloured background
  • Choosing the wrong category — e.g. e-Business for actual employment
  • Passport validity under six months, or details typed differently from the passport
  • Previous Indian citizenship not disclosed — former Indian citizens may need a surrender certificate

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert an e-Tourist visa to another category inside India?

Generally no. e-Visas are non-extendable and non-convertible in most cases; apply for the right category before you travel.

I’m of Indian origin — should I get an e-Visa or an OCI?

If you travel to India often and are eligible, an OCI saves repeated applications. If your trip is soon, an e-Visa is usually faster while your OCI is processed.

Is the e-Arrival card mandatory?

Check the official portal for the current requirement for your nationality and port of entry. It is free to submit online.
