OCI eligibility check

Six yes/no questions. You’ll get an instant, plain-English answer and the documents to start gathering.

1. Are you currently a citizen of a country other than India?
2. Were you, a parent, grandparent or great-grandparent ever an Indian citizen (on/after 26 Jan 1950), or from territory that became part of India after 1947?
3. Are you married to an Indian citizen or OCI cardholder, with a registered marriage of 2+ years?
4. Are or were you, or any parent/grandparent/great-grandparent, a citizen of Pakistan or Bangladesh?
5. Are you serving in, or retired from, a foreign military?
6. Have you ever held an Indian passport?

Informal screening based on the publicly listed grounds in the OCI guide. It is not a legal determination.
