Photo rules that trip people up

  • Square only. Online OCI and e-Visa uploads expect equal width and height.
  • Plain, light background with no shadows behind the head.
  • File limits. Portals set minimum and maximum dimensions and file sizes — commonly cited for OCI uploads are at least 200×200 px and no more than 500 KB, in JPEG.
  • Printed photos for passport applications abroad are usually 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) on a white background.

Specifications can change and vary by mission. The portal you upload to has the final say.
