How the finder works

The finder maps your citizenship status and goal to the consular service that applies. It follows the basic rules: Indian citizens use Indian passports; foreign citizens need a visa or OCI; former Indian citizens need a surrender certificate before an OCI. Edge cases — dual-nationality children, lost documents, old PIO cards — are where a free specialist review helps most.
